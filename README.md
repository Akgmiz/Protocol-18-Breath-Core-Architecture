## The Science of Protocol 18 & Breath-Core
While Aegis Corp created 17 cyber-assassins (**Protocols 01 to 17**) using forced neural chips running at a memory-erasing **160 BPM overdrive**, **Subject 18 (Kael)** is an unchipped clone of Aegis's original Chief Scientist[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). Kael utilizes **Breath-Core**—an ancient, pre-Aegis bio-rhythmic science based on **Core Rhythm** and **18 Nodal Points (18 Locks)** in the human nervous system[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

## The 18-Lock Power-Scaling Table (Heartbeat Biometrics)
* **72 BPM (Dormant / Deep Reset):** Normal human baseline[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span). Kael's Tactical Neural Respirator remains unlocked around his neck[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* **142 BPM (Tactical Overclock - Locks 1 to 6):** The exact biometric threshold where Kael's mask magnetically snaps onto his face (glowing **Cyan** for a free mind, contrasted against Aegis's **Red** chipped control)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span). At 142 BPM, the bone-conduction AI recording of the original scientist—known as **ZERO**—awakens in Kael's ears[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span). Has a strict **180-second (3-minute) burnout limit** before causing neural damage and *Neural Echo*[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
* **18 Hz Phantom Frequency (Lock 3):** Utilizing acoustic infrasound resonance to disrupt inner-ear balance sensors and induce visual hallucinations in combat.
* **160 BPM (Phantom State - Locks 7 to 12):** Used by **Protocol 01 (Vane)**[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span). Costs one permanent memory per use (*Memory Decay*)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
* **180 BPM (Berserk / Forbidden Zone - Locks 13 to 17):** Dual eye state (one Cyan, one Red) where ZERO takes full physical control of Kael's body[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).
* **38 BPM (Living Hibernation) & 0 BPM (Lock 18 - Dead Calm God State):** Extreme biometric states used to evade orbital heat-tracking grids and interface directly with the **Mother-Core**[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).
